Play Zones

Nine zones, one island,
endless routes through it

From the ball-pit fort to the quiet craft booth — here is everything waiting inside Treasure Island, and who each corner is built for.

Themed zones
Year and up
Hours open daily
Supervised play
Grip socks are required in every play zone — for children and for any adult going inside with them. Forgot yours? They are available at reception.
Zone 01

The Play Fort

Our biggest structure — three levels of tunnels, tube slides, rope bridges and a ball pit deep enough to vanish into. It is the beating heart of the island and where most visits begin.

  • Multi-level climbing frame with soft-padded walls
  • Twin tube slides and a wobble bridge
  • Ball pit with thousands of sanitised balls
  • Play hosts stationed at the entry and exit points
Ages 3–10
Zone 02

Jump Bay

Side-by-side trampolines framed by padded walls and safety netting. Kids queue up, bounce out their energy and come back grinning — it is the quickest reset button we know.

  • Two full-size trampoline beds with netted surround
  • Padded frame covers on every edge
  • One jumper per bed, supervised at all times
  • Grip socks required
Ages 4 and up
Zone 03

Create & Learn

Two booths built for making things. CREATE is our craft corner — paint, glue, glitter and whatever the week's project happens to be. LEARN is the quieter side, with puzzles, books and table games.

  • Hosted craft sessions running through the day
  • Seasonal projects that change every week
  • Take-home artwork for every little maker
  • Wipe-clean everything — bring your mess
All ages
Zone 04

The Lighthouse

Our landmark. A red-and-white tower rising out of the middle of the island with a spiral climb inside and a lookout at the top. If you lose track of your child, start here — everyone does.

  • Enclosed spiral climb with hand-holds throughout
  • Soft landing mat at the base
  • Lookout window over the whole play floor
  • The best photo spot in the venue
Ages 3 and up
Zone 05

Pirates' Cove

A pedal-kart track running along a hand-painted reef wall, with treasure barrels, a picket fence and a long padded bench for parents. Traffic jams are frequent and very loud.

  • Fleet of pedal karts and ride-on vehicles
  • Hand-painted reef wall with sea creatures to spot
  • Cushioned crash mats along the full length
  • Seating right beside the track
Ages 2–8
Zone 06

Toddler Lagoon

A gated, low-level world kept separate from the bigger kids. Soft blocks, bouncy animals, mirrors and textures — everything at crawling height and everything washable.

  • Fully gated and separated from the main floor
  • Foam blocks, tunnels and ride-on animals
  • Nothing higher than a toddler can safely climb
  • Parents welcome inside the gate
Ages 1–3
Zone 07

Role-Play Village

Little houses with striped awnings and open windows where children run a shop, a clinic, a kitchen or a ship. It is where the quiet ones come alive and the loud ones learn to take turns.

  • Themed play houses with real working props
  • Dress-up basket restocked daily
  • Encourages language, sharing and imaginative play
  • Popular with siblings of different ages
Ages 2–7
Zone 08

The Boutique

Our little shop by the entrance — toys, books, keepsakes and party favours. You do not need a play ticket to browse, and it is the traditional last stop before going home.

  • Toys, plush and books hand-picked for the age range
  • Party favours and gift sets for birthdays
  • Treasure Island keepsakes you cannot buy elsewhere
  • Open the same hours as the play area
Open to everyone

House rules

Keeping the island safe

A short list, strictly kept — it is what lets us say yes to everything else.

Grip socks on

Required for every child and every adult entering a play zone. Available at reception.

An adult stays on site

Treasure Island is not a drop-off centre. A parent or guardian remains in the venue throughout.

Age zones respected

The toddler area is reserved for under-threes so our smallest guests never get flattened.

Well means well

Please keep unwell children home. We sanitise daily, but we cannot do it mid-sneeze.

Food stays outside play

Eating and drinking happen in the café and party areas, never on the equipment.

Photograph your own

Snap away at your own children — please be considerate of other families in frame.

Come and explore it yourself

Walk in any day between 10 AM and 10 PM — no booking needed for open play.
