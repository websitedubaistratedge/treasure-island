Programs

Learning that looks
a lot like playing

Weekly playgroup sessions, creative workshops and school-holiday camps — all run by our team, all built around how children actually learn.

Playgroup

A gentle first step
into school life

Our weekday playgroup gives little ones a structured morning away from home — in small groups, with the same familiar faces each week. It is built for the year or two before nursery, when confidence matters more than curriculum.

  • Small groups so every child gets attention and a turn.
  • A predictable rhythm — welcome circle, active play, craft, story, goodbye song.
  • Social skills first — sharing, waiting, joining in, saying goodbye at the door.
  • Weekday mornings, with flexible weekly or termly blocks.
Get the schedule
A typical session

Ninety minutes, five parts

The shape stays the same every week. The theme is what changes.

1

Welcome circle

Names, a hello song and a look at what today is about. It settles the room and marks the start.

2

Active play

Free run of the play zones with the host joining in — climbing, balancing, chasing, burning it off.

3

Make something

A craft or sensory activity tied to the week's theme. It always goes home with them.

4

Snack and sit

Everyone at the table together — a small but real lesson in patience and conversation.

5

Story and goodbye

A book, a song and a proper goodbye so the ending is never a surprise.

Workshops

Something new every week

Drop-in creative sessions hosted in the CREATE booth. Themes are announced on Instagram each week.

Art & craft

Painting, collage, clay and seasonal making — messy on purpose, and cleaned up by us.

Sensory play

Textures, water, sand and light for the youngest hands. Calming, absorbing, slightly chaotic.

Little chefs

No-bake cooking sessions where children measure, mix and eat what they made.

Music & movement

Instruments, rhythm games and dancing — the loudest half hour of our week.

Story time

Read-aloud sessions with props and puppets, aimed at the pre-reading age.

Career weeks

Themed weeks where the role-play village becomes a bakery, a clinic or a fire station.

Holiday camps

Every school break,
covered

When school stops, the island opens wider. Half-day and full-day camps run through the winter, spring and summer breaks with a different theme each week — so children can come back for more than one and never repeat a day.

  • Half-day and full-day options
  • A new theme every week of the break
  • Craft, active play, games and a supervised lunch break
  • Sibling and multi-week rates available
