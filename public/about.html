About us

Built for the way
children actually play

Treasure Island is a kids' playground and edutainment centre inside Galeries Lafayette at The Dubai Mall — where children play, learn and explore through fun activities.

Our story

A small island in the
middle of a big mall

The Dubai Mall is not short of things to do. What it was short of was somewhere a two-year-old and a nine-year-old could both be happy for two hours, within sight of a parent holding a coffee.

So we built one. Treasure Island opened on the second level of Galeries Lafayette with a simple brief: make it genuinely safe, make it beautiful enough that adults want to stay, and fill it with things that teach something without ever announcing that they are teaching.

The pirate theme was never decoration for its own sake. An island gives children a story to be inside — a lighthouse to climb, a cove to race through, a ship to sail. Nine zones, one narrative, and a floor plan that works whether you have ninety minutes or a whole afternoon.

What we believe

Four things we won't compromise on

Safety is the product

Padded surfaces, age-separated zones, daily sanitising and hosts on the floor — not a policy on a wall.

Play teaches

Every zone has something underneath it — balance, sequencing, negotiation, patience, language.

Parents matter too

Comfortable seating, clear sightlines and good coffee a few steps away. You are a guest, not a supervisor.

It should be beautiful

Hand-painted murals, real greenery and a build quality that holds up. Children notice care.

Safety

The unglamorous part
we care most about

Every parent walks in doing the same quick scan. Here is what you will find when you do.

  • Soft, padded flooring throughout every play zone
  • Under-threes kept in their own gated area
  • Equipment checked daily and sanitised on a fixed schedule
  • Trained play hosts on the floor during all opening hours
  • Grip socks required — no exceptions, including for adults
  • An adult stays on site: we are not a drop-off centre
  • Capacity managed so zones never get dangerously crowded
