Getting here

We're easier to find than you'd think

By car

Park in Fashion Avenue or Grand Parking, head to Galeries Lafayette and take the escalator to Level 2.

By metro

Burj Khalifa / Dubai Mall station on the Red Line, then the air-conditioned link bridge into the mall.

Once inside

Find Galeries Lafayette, go up to the second level and follow signs for Le Gourmet. We're the blue clubhouse.
