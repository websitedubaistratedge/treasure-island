Our signature celebration

The Royal Birthday

This is the one we are known for. The birthday child arrives to a red carpet rolled out at the entrance, lined with gold stanchions, velvet ropes and their own portraits in gold frames. They walk it, they are announced, and they step into a room dressed head to toe in the theme they chose.

Our crew runs the entire day — the welcome, the games, the cake, the photographs and the clean-up. Parents are guests at their own child’s party.

How it works

Booked in four messages

1

Send us the date

WhatsApp or call with your preferred date, the birthday child's age and a rough guest count. We'll confirm availability straight away.

2

Pick a package and a theme

We'll send the current rate card and walk you through the theme options, food choices and any add-ons you want.

3

Confirm with a deposit

A deposit secures your slot. We'll send a written confirmation with the full run-sheet for the day.

4

Arrive fifteen minutes early

Everything will already be set up. Hand us the cake, take a breath, and enjoy the party.
